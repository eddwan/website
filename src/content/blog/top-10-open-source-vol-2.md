## A lista, do 10º ao 1º

### 10. UseAgent

Colega de IA com computador, navegador e terminal, no seu servidor.
[github.com/useagenthq/useagent](https://github.com/useagenthq/useagent)

### 9. Openship

Deploy self-hosted com CI/CD, roteamento e HTTPS.
[github.com/oblien/openship](https://github.com/oblien/openship)

### 8. Expense Budget Tracker

Controle de gastos self-hosted, com o seu agente de IA lançando os extratos.
[github.com/kirill-markin/expense-budget-tracker](https://github.com/kirill-markin/expense-budget-tracker)

### 7. Note67

Notas de reunião com transcrição e resumo por IA, tudo local.
[github.com/ZapYap-com/note67](https://github.com/ZapYap-com/note67)

### 6. BookOrbit

Biblioteca self-hosted de ebooks, PDFs, audiobooks e quadrinhos.
[github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit)

### 5. Agent Orchestrator

Agentes de código em paralelo com Kanban de PRs, CI e revisões.
[github.com/Untrivial-ai/agent-orchestrator](https://github.com/Untrivial-ai/agent-orchestrator)

### 4. App Store Screenshots

Skill de agente que cria screenshots para App Store e Google Play.
[github.com/ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots](https://github.com/ParthJadhav/app-store-screenshots)

### 3. Claude Pulse

Barra do Claude Code com uso da sessão, da semana e custo.
[github.com/NoobyGains/claude-pulse](https://github.com/NoobyGains/claude-pulse)

### 2. Dockhand

Interface moderna para Docker: containers, Compose, deploy via Git.
[github.com/Finsys/dockhand](https://github.com/Finsys/dockhand)

### 1. crawlseo
